About me

My interests used to change all the time. I experimented with Discord servers, spent about six months speedcubing, and then started learning Python, C#, Unity, and 3D modeling.

Later I became interested in entrepreneurship. I started reading and learning about business while taking strength training more seriously. In 2026, I added boxing. That summer I built and launched IdeaNest, my first independent project in this area. It didn’t develop as I hoped, but it was valuable hands-on experience.

These days, I keep training and boxing, studying entrepreneurship and English. I still enjoy 3D modeling in my spare time, read self-development books, and follow cars and finance. I also work on small everyday skills, from touch typing to reaction speed.
